Artefak
- crates/domain/src/mfa.rs  parameter RFC 6238 (SHA-1, 6 digit, 30 detik, ±1 langkah), base32 RFC 4648, parse kode
  (6 digit = TOTP; 10 karakter base32 = kode pemulihan, `-`/spasi/huruf besar diterima), jendela + anti-replay
  (langkah ≤ terakhir diterima ditolak), URI otpauth, kebijakan platform_requires_mfa / tenant_requires_mfa.
- crates/infrastructure/src/mfa.rs  HOTP/TOTP HMAC-SHA1 (semua kandidat dihitung, tanpa keluar awal), rahasia 160-bit
  dienkripsi envelope ADR-0011 (AAD terikat user_id), 10 kode pemulihan (hash SHA-256, sekali pakai), tantangan login
  (hash token, TTL 5 menit, 5 percobaan, sekali pakai), enable mencabut sesi lain + menandai sesi ini mfa_verified.
- migrations/0026_mfa.{up,down}.sql  kolom envelope + last_used_step di mfa_credentials, mfa_recovery_codes,
  mfa_challenges, user_sessions.mfa_verified_at, tenants.require_admin_mfa.
- apps/api/src/mfa.rs  POST /auth/login/mfa; GET /auth/mfa; POST /auth/mfa/{setup,enable,disable,recovery-codes};
  GET/PUT /tenants/{t}/security. /auth/login → 202 {mfa_required, mfa_token} tanpa cookie bila MFA aktif.
- apps/api/src/session.rs  SessionUser menolak sesi role platform wajib MFA yang belum lolos (403 reason=mfa_required);
  MfaPendingUser hanya untuk status/setup/enable MFA, sesi, logout. Member menolak role Owner/Manager/Admin di tenant
  yang mewajibkan MFA bila sesi belum lolos MFA.
- Audit: auth.mfa.enabled/disabled/failed/recovery_regenerated, tenant.security_updated (before/after).
- Rate limit: mfa_ip 30/15 menit, mfa_user 10/15 menit (fail-closed).
- Dependency baru: sha1 0.11.0 (docs/architecture/dependency-review.md); cargo deny check: advisories/bans/licenses/sources ok.
